About the school

STEM schools in Egypt are specialized secondary schools for talented students. STEM Damietta School follows this model, encouraging project-based learning, laboratory work, research and teamwork.

🎯

Our mission

Prepare creative problem-solvers who can contribute to Egypt's future in science and technology.

👁️

Our vision

A generation of innovators who think critically, work together and turn ideas into solutions.

🤝

Our values

Curiosity, integrity, collaboration and a love of learning.

Admission

Admission to Egyptian STEM schools is competitive and usually includes a placement exam and a personal interview after middle school.

Requirements, dates and fees are set by the Ministry of Education and change each year. Please check the official announcements for the current details.
